# 01 · Project Vision

This is the long-term vision for NOV as a press kit and creative ecosystem.

## Project goals

- Position NOV as a curator of journeys rather than a club performer.
- Build a premium press kit website that reads like an editorial presentation.
- Create a narrative structure anchored in timecodes, not conventional pages.
- Support booking and press discovery through a calm, intentional digital experience.
- Keep every design and content decision aligned with the existing direction.

## Emotional goals

The website should feel:

- Curated
- Mysterious
- Confident
- Elegant
- Calm
- Intentional
- Trustworthy

## Audience

- Promoters and booking agents.
- Creative directors and editors.
- Music programmers and label curators.
- Press, radio, and festival bookers.
- Listeners who value depth and restraint.

## What success looks like

- The visitor understands NOV as a taste maker before hearing a track.
- The site feels premium, not loud.
- The press kit is easy to navigate and feels useful.
- Booking feels natural and respectful.
- The project maintains consistency across design, motion, photography and copy.

## First 5 seconds

The first impression should be:

- A quiet, museum-like entry.
- A sense of editorial intent.
- A clear, bold typographic identity.
- Curiosity rather than being sold to.
- A subtle invitation to continue.

## After leaving

The visitor should feel:

- NOV is serious and deliberate.
- The artist is selective and thoughtful.
- The experience was refined and memorable.
- This is not a generic DJ page.

## What promoters should think

- NOV values atmosphere, not volume.
- Booking NOV is a choice in taste and curation.
- The press kit is practical and premium.
- This artist brings intention to every room.
