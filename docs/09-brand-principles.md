# 09 · Brand Principles

## Core identity

- NOV is a curator.
- Taste before performance.
- Selection before quantity.
- Silence is part of the journey.
- Less is more.
- Timeless over trendy.
- Emotion before technology.
- Typography before decoration.

## Visual principles

- Black is the ground.
- Soft white is the accent.
- One deep red is the signal.
- Use multiple blacks, not a single flat black.
- Avoid pure white and loud color.
- Avoid gradients unless nearly invisible.
- Use negative space as structure.

## Motion principles

- Motion should feel intentional.
- Crossfades are the default.
- Stillness is stronger than movement.
- Only animate when it adds clarity.

## Content principles

- The experience is a journey.
- Each section answers one question.
- Every page element must have purpose.
- The site should feel like an editorial narrative.

## Copy principles

- Precise and minimal.
- Editorial, not promotional.
- Calm, not loud.
- Avoid marketing language.
- Avoid influencer-style phrasing.

## Brand promise

- NOV selects deeply and thoughtfully.
- The experience is curated, not manufactured.
- The press kit gives confidence without overselling.
