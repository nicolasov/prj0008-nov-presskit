# 02 · Creative Direction

This document is the project constitution. It records every decision that has shaped NOV’s identity and governs future work.

## Core proposition

- Museum, not club.
- NOV is a curator, not a performer.
- The website is an editorial experience, not a portfolio.
- The press kit must feel intentional, restrained and timeless.
- Typography and space are the primary visual language.
- Every choice must serve the journey.

## Influences and tone

- Apple 70%: clarity, precision, spatial calm.
- Leica 30%: photographic restraint, texture, low-key light.
- A24: cinematic rhythm and pacing.
- Kinfolk: editorial breath and stillness.
- Tarantino pacing: chaptered narrative, deliberate reveals.
- Mystery, elegance, respect, calm.
- Curated Journeys as the central concept.
- The Journey is the structure: eight cues, one runtime.

## Hero philosophy

- The hero is the emotional center.
- It should read like an introduction to a gallery or a theater program.
- The word "NOV" is a museum title projected into space.
- The hero reveal is gradual and atmospheric.
- The hero establishes the set's tone before content begins.

## NOV reveal

- NOV is presented as light developing from darkness.
- The reveal is subtle, not theatrical.
- The only motion in the hero is atmospheric and purposeful.
- The DOM fallback is always present for accessibility and performance.

## Red pigment

- One deep red is the signal.
- It marks where the visitor is and where they can act.
- It is never decorative.
- It occupies a tiny portion of the page, never more than a whisper of the palette.

## Typography as primary visual language

- Newsreader is the narrator voice. It is rare, light and editorial.
- Archivo is the structure voice. It drives headings, navigation and body.
- Geist Mono is the metadata voice for timecodes and captions.
- Typography is the strongest visual asset. Decoration must never override it.

## Negative space and timelessness

- Negative space is the frame for every idea.
- The design uses space to make content breathe.
- The project chooses timeless restraint over trend.
- The work should feel durable, not momentary.

## Journey structure

- Eight sections correspond to eight cues.
- Timecodes are editorial markers, not actual durations.
- Navigation behaves like a tracklist in a press kit.
- Motion is a series of crossfades and transitions, not an animation show.

## Things we never do

- Never EDM aesthetics.
- Never glassmorphism.
- Never generic gradients.
- Never influencer design.
- Never excessive WebGL.
- Never smoke effects that are decorative or obvious.
- Never unnecessary animations.
- Never animate only because it looks cool.
- Never communicate ego.
- Never oversell.
- Never use marketing language.
- Never use fake HDR.
- Never oversaturate photography.
- Never generic galleries.
- Never sacrifice typography for decoration.
